Present Simple

Use the present simple for habits, routines and facts.

EnglishMeaning / useExample
I / you / we / they + verbBase form of the verbI work in a shop.
he / she / it + verb-sAdd -s (or -es)She works in a bank.
do / does + notNegativeHe doesn't like coffee.
Do / Does ...?QuestionsDo you live here?
always, usually, sometimes, neverFrequency adverbsI usually walk to school.

Good to know

  • Frequency adverbs go before the main verb but after "be": "He is always late."
Practice: Describe your morning routine in five sentences using frequency adverbs.
