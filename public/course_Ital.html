Syllabus

• Week 1: First Steps & Phonetics • Grammar: The alphabet, pronunciation rules, and subject pronouns (io, tu, noi). • Speaking: Greetings, goodbyes, and introducing yourself. • Week 2: Essential Verbs & Identity • Grammar: The core verbs essere (to be) and avere (to have). Numbers 1–30. • Speaking: Asking and telling age, nationality, profession, and phone numbers. • Week 3: Nouns, Articles & Objects • Grammar: Gender and number of nouns (masculine/feminine, singular/plural). Definite and indefinite articles. • Speaking: Describing everyday objects and colors. • Week 4: Daily Life (Regular Verbs) • Grammar: Present tense of regular verbs ending in -are, -ere, and -ire. Days of the week. • Speaking: Talking about your daily routine, hobbies, and free time. • Week 5: Navigating the City • Grammar: Basic prepositions (in, a, da) and irregular verbs of movement (andare, venire). • Speaking: Asking for directions, taking public transit, and identifying city landmarks. • Week 6: At the Cafe & Restaurant • Grammar: Modal verbs (volere, potere, dovere) and the polite phrase Vorrei... (I would like...). • Speaking: Ordering food and drinks, paying the bill, and expressing food preferences. • Week 7: Family & Descriptions • Grammar: Possessive adjectives (mio, tuo, etc.) and descriptive adjectives. • Speaking: Describing your family members, physical appearance, and personality traits. • Week 8: Shopping & Final Review • Grammar: Numbers up to 100+. Review of all core concepts. • Speaking: Asking for prices and sizes in a clothing store or market. Final conversational practice.
